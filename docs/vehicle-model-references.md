## Modeling policy

- Match the production car's wheelbase, overall dimensions, greenhouse placement, and axle positions first.
- Generate one six-view sheet per exact model year and trim, then constrain the mesh to published dimensions instead of tracing AI pixels as measurements.
- Preserve paintable body surfaces and selectable modifications as independent geometry.
- Use photographic details as references only; do not use a photograph as a texture or ship it as an application asset.
- Keep a visible disclaimer because the viewer is a configuration preview, not manufacturer CAD or an engineering fitment guarantee.
